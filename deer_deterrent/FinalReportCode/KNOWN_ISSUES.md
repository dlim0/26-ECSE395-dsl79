# Known issues and future iteration considerations

This folder records the last course implementation, not a claim that field validation is complete. See [current state](CURRENT_STATE_2026-11-23.md) for exact deployed rules.

- **Surrogate detection, not deer recognition.** The default vibration_fusion uses PIR plus wireless vibration followed by image-region confirmation. The older five-frame growth rule remains in classifier_test, not the deployed default. Actual deer detection and behavioral deterrence have not been validated.
- **Limited nighttime contrast.** The user reported a completely black jacket failed, while cardboard succeeded. A recorded illuminated hand triggered via grayscale at7.271% and7.688%; assisted scores9.354% and9.417% stayed below10%. Color detection cannot recover an object that remains indistinguishable from black. Black-fabric reflectance and flash/exposure settling are possible factors, not established causes.
- **Fixed baseline and mode.** Mode is chosen once from12 calibration frames: at least75% of blocks below20/255 enables color assistance. It does not automatically switch at sunset. Recalibrate after camera movement or substantial lighting changes; keep target and hands out of the baseline. Median brightness compensation does not remove every shadow, exposure, or white-balance change.
- **Provisional thresholds.** Original grayscale contrast25, area7%; assisted area10%; two consecutive qualifying frames. These are image-area thresholds, not physical size or identity. Dark gate and chroma threshold require broader controls. Offline JPEG replays with three saved baseline images are not field performance estimates.
- **Power and capture reliability.** Earlier repeated malformed/incomplete JPEGs stopped in monitored startup captures after the user secured a reportedly loose3.3V connection and reset. This supports a wiring/power explanation but does not prove causality or long-duration stability. Initialization alone does not verify image quality.
- **PIR supply and interference.** Separate battery power with shared ground was user-tested. Warm-up60s; trigger HIGH100ms; LOW500ms rearm. Radiator-facing placement and power changes were associated with earlier irregular behavior; exact causes were not isolated. Post-alarm cooldown4.5s is mitigation, not proof of electrical immunity.
- **Wireless dependency.** Wi-Fi is ON in vibration_fusion. Qualifying vibration must arrive during the10s PIR window; missing confirmation leaves camera in standby. MCU remains awake. No current/runtime, packet-loss, range, or weatherproofing validation is available.
- **Light/speaker.** White RGB illumination is not a calibrated photographic strobe. Current capture waits120ms and discards two frames; longer illumination was proposed but not implemented/tested. Alarm8kHz at50% duty drivesGPIO10; logs establish commanded operation, not acoustic level or deer response. No sound-pressure measurement exists.
- **Wiring and TFT.** Source camera D3 maps toGPIO11; D1 maps toGPIO12. OV5640 module silkscreen naming may differ; use source mapping and WIRING.md. GPIO35/36 are shared with TFT; keep TFT disabled while RGB uses them. Images can be sideways.
- **Evidence gaps.** Outdoor surrogate observations are now available, but target distance, trial denominators, coat diversity, battery runtime, and long-duration false-alarm rate are not. Preserve failed trials and distinguish user reports from serial-confirmed captures. Synthetic survey examples are not participant data.

Future work: measured-distance brown/gray/black targets, empty-scene/wind/shadow controls, consistent flash-exposure comparison, gradual baseline adaptation outside detection windows, power integrity and battery measurements, then ethically planned actual-animal observation. No further implementation is claimed for this course iteration.
